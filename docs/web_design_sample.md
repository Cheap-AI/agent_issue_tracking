# MVP Requirements (v0.1)

## Primary Goal

Provide the clearest possible view of the world's most important Issues.

The homepage should answer:

> "What matters right now?"

within 10 seconds.

---

# Core Principles

* Visibility over complexity
* Understanding over information
* Facts over opinions
* Issues over news
* Simplicity over features

---

# MVP Pages

## 1. Home

Purpose:
Show the current World Issue Index.

Contents:

* Top Issues
* Importance Score
* Trend (↑ ↓ →)
* Last Updated
* Search

---

## 2. Issue Detail

Each Issue contains only:

Title

Importance

Why it Matters (1 sentence)

What's Changing (1–3 bullet points)

Related Issues

Recent Updates (timeline)

Related News (links only)

---

## 3. Search

Simple search by Issue name.

No AI chat in MVP.

---

# Issue Card

Each card should display:

Issue Name

Importance

Trend

One-line "Why it Matters"

Example:

---

AI Infrastructure ↑

Importance: 8.8

Why it matters

Determines the speed and cost of AI development.

Updated 2 hours ago

---

---

# Navigation

Minimal.

Home

Search

Bookmarks (optional)

Settings

Nothing else.

---

# Visual Style

Clean

Minimal

Professional

Modern

Comfortable whitespace

Low visual noise

No excessive colors.

Use color only to communicate meaning.

---

# Information Hierarchy

Users should notice:

1. Issue Name

2. Importance

3. Trend

4. Why it Matters

Everything else is secondary.

---

# User Experience Goals

A user should be able to:

Understand today's important Issues in under 60 seconds.

Find a specific Issue in under 10 seconds.

Understand why an Issue matters without reading long paragraphs.

Leave the app feeling informed rather than overwhelmed.

---

# Things NOT Included

No social feed

No comments

No long AI conversations

No predictions

No gamification

No notifications

No personalization

No complicated dashboards

Keep the MVP focused.

---

# Success Criteria

Users say:

"I immediately understood what was important."

"I didn't have to read a lot."

"The interface feels calm."

"I want to check it again tomorrow."

Not:

"This has a lot of features."
