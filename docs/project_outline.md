# Project Manifesto (Draft v0.1)

## Vision

Create the world's clearest interface for understanding what matters.

Not another news app.
Not another AI chatbot.

A living map of the world's most important issues.

---

# Core Philosophy

The modern problem is not the lack of information.

It is the inability to organize, prioritize, and understand it.

Our goal is to reduce information overload by transforming thousands of daily events into a structured, evolving set of Issues.

Users should leave feeling:

* "I understand what changed."
* "My time was well spent."
* "The world makes more sense."

---

# What is an Issue?

An Issue is not simply news.

An Issue is a meaningful state, trend, conflict, opportunity, or change that shapes the world over time.

Examples:

* AI Infrastructure
* China's Growth
* Climate Change
* Global Energy
* Semiconductor Supply Chain
* Healthcare Innovation

News comes and goes.

Issues evolve.

---

# Product Goal

Maintain a continuously updated World Issue Index.

Instead of tracking individual articles, the system tracks Issues.

Each Issue represents the current state of an important part of the world.

---

# AI's Role

AI is not the product.

AI is the engine behind the product.

Responsibilities include:

* Collect information
* Research developments
* Track Issue changes
* Connect related events
* Detect emerging Issues
* Update importance
* Summarize meaningful changes

The user interacts with Issues, not with raw AI output.

---

# Human's Role

Humans improve the system.

Possible contributions:

* Suggest missing Issues
* Suggest why an Issue matters
* Recommend related Issues
* Surface overlooked developments

The community complements AI rather than competing with it.

---

# Information Principles

Every piece of information should answer:

What is it?

Why does it matter?

What changed?

Nothing more unless the user asks.

---

# Design Principles

The interface should feel:

* Clear
* Calm
* Intelligent
* Timeless
* Trustworthy

Never overwhelming.

Never noisy.

Never sensational.

The UI should create the feeling of cognitive relief.

Users should immediately understand the state of the world.

---

# User Experience

The experience should feel like:

"I opened the app for one minute and now I understand today's world."

The goal is not maximum engagement.

The goal is maximum clarity.

---

# Personality

Professional.

Objective.

Respectful.

Curious.

Minimal.

Never dramatic.

Never clickbait.

Never emotionally manipulative.

---

# Trust

Trust is earned through consistency.

Every Issue should evolve logically over time.

Whenever something changes, users should understand why.

Authority comes from transparent reasoning rather than confident language.

---

# Long-term Vision

Become the world's living issue map.

Not a news platform.

Not a social network.

Not an AI chatbot.

A shared understanding of the world's most important Issues.

Continuously updated by AI.

Improved by people.

Designed to help humanity better understand itself.
