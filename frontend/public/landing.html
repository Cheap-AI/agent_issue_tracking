— 01 / SYNOPSIS

The sun went dark.
Humanity set out for the end.

The year is 2147. As the sun slowly cools, humanity has just nine years left. From the edge of the galaxy, where every trace of starlight fades, an unidentified signal arrives from the End of Stars.

The last seven crew of the survey ship Astra set out on a course with no return. What waits beyond: a new beginning, or the face of the ending we have refused to see?

At the farthest place, they will face the nearest truth.
